The Compute Wall

Why edge AI power budgets break before deployment.

Data-movement energy tax

Classical von Neumann NPUs spend over 80% of their total power budget shuttling weights between off-chip DRAM and the compute fabric — not performing arithmetic. At 3-7 pJ/bit, memory access costs 100× more than a multiply-accumulate operation.

Thermal & battery constraints

Tactical drones, EV ADAS systems, and soldier-wearable sensors operate under strict SWaP-C limits. A 50–100 W NPU draw eliminates viable flight time, reduces EV range, and requires heavy active cooling that defeats the purpose of edge deployment.

Tape-out risk without simulation

Physical mixed-signal silicon tape-outs run ₹5–50Cr per iteration. Committing to a hardware BOM before validating model behavior under analog noise, thermal drift, and quantization degradation is operationally unacceptable.
