THE INDUSTRY CHALLENGE

The Compute Wall at the Edge

Standard digital NPUs and GPUs hit a hard physical wall when running deep vision and attention models on edge devices.

01 / ENERGY TAX

100x Data Movement Tax

Von Neumann digital architectures drag weights constantly between separate DRAM/SRAM and compute logic, spending over 80% of total energy purely on data shuttling rather than math.

02 / THERMAL BARRIER

Thermal & Battery Limits

Tactical drones, EV ADAS perception units, and soldier-wearable sensors cannot afford heavy cooling fans or 100W+ power draws without drastically degrading operational range.

03 / SILICON RISKS

Tape-Out Risk & BOM Cost

Committing to physical mixed-signal silicon tape-outs without verifying hardware noise bounds risks multi-million dollar spin iterations when models fail in real silicon.
